Trades per 5 minutes in each hour, your time. The blue line is today, the amber line the last comparable day, and the dashed line a typical day (the median of each hour). Volume and trades by weekday
—

Bars are USDT volume per 5 minutes (left axis), with the brightest bar the day with the most volume. The line is trades per 5 minutes (right axis). Each day pools all 24 hours, so this shows which days are busier overall, not when during the day. A day whose line holds up while its bar drops has activity but less money behind it, as on weekends.

Each cell is the median (or mean) of trades or volume per 5 minutes, whichever is selected above, for one weekday and one hour of your time. Brighter cells are busier. Today and the current hour are highlighted, and the outlined cell is now, so reading along the row to the right shows what usually comes next. The colour scale ignores the most extreme 5% at each end, so one wild day does not wash out the rest. Faded cells rest on fewer than three days of data, so trust them less. Hover a cell for the exact number. The coloured strip at the top marks the US session phases, as in the legend above.

Activity by Hour

Volume and trades per 5 min by hour of day, across all days in the period

Bars are USDT volume per 5 minutes (left axis); the three brightest are the busiest hours. The line is the number of trades per 5 minutes (right axis). Both axes start at zero, so compare their shapes: where the line sits high but the bars are low, there are lots of trades but little money behind them, which means small trades and a thin market. Where the bars rise with the line, the size is there too. The shading marks the US session phases in your time. Hover an hour for both numbers.
