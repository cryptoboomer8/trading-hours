Activity by Hour

Volume and trades per 5 min by hour of day, across all days in the period

Bars are USDT volume per 5 minutes (left axis); the three brightest are the busiest hours. The line is the number of trades per 5 minutes (right axis). Both axes start at zero, so compare their shapes: where the line sits high but the bars are low, there are lots of trades but little money behind them, which means small trades and a thin market. Where the bars rise with the line, the size is there too. The shading marks the US session phases in your time. Hover an hour for both numbers.

Day-over-Day

Volume and trades per 5 min by day of week, across all days in the period

Bars are USDT volume per 5 minutes (left axis), with the brightest bar the day with the most volume. The line is trades per 5 minutes (right axis). Each day pools all 24 hours, so it tells you which days are busier overall, not when during the day. A weekday whose line holds up while its bar drops has activity but less money behind it.
